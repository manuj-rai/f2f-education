## Course page

**How innerwear fabrics are designed to deliver skincare ingredients, and how to judge the claims**

Underwear is starting to borrow from the skincare shelf. In this free readable course, based on the Fibre2Fashion article by Kshipra Kulkarni, you'll learn what cosmetotextiles are, how ingredients such as hyaluronic acid, vitamins and fragrances are built into fibres, finishes and microcapsules, and why innerwear is such a natural platform for them. You'll also see why an ingredient in a fabric does not automatically work like a cosmetic product, how claims can be tested, why washing matters, and what intimate skin safety demands. Three short lessons, a knowledge check and a free certificate.

**Who it is for:** Innerwear designers, product developers, merchandisers, students and textile professionals interested in functional finishes and where fabrics meet personal care.

**You will learn to:**
- Explain what cosmetotextiles are and how they deliver ingredients
- Compare fibre incorporation, coatings and microencapsulation as delivery routes
- Explain why innerwear suits beauty-enhanced fabric development
- Identify the factors that decide whether a cosmetotextile performs
- Describe the testing, wash-durability and skin-safety checks responsible products need

**Source:** this course is based on the Fibre2Fashion article [When Innerwear Meets Skincare: The Rise of Beauty Benefit-Enhanced Fabrics](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/11918/when-innerwear-meets-skincare-the-rise-of-beauty-benefit-enhanced-fabrics) by Kshipra Kulkarni (Nov 2026).
