## Course page

**How layered, high-performance fabrics keep astronauts safe, and where space clothing is heading next**

A spacesuit is not one fabric but a wearable life-support system built from carefully engineered layers. In this free readable course, based on the Fibre2Fashion article by Diti Shah, you'll learn why space is so hostile to the human body, which high-performance fibres go into spacesuits and why, and how suits manage heat, radiation, fast-moving particles and the need to move freely. You'll also look ahead to lighter, smarter and more sustainable garments for long missions to the Moon and Mars, and see how space research can inspire clothing on Earth. Four short lessons, a knowledge check and a free certificate.

**Who it is for:** Students, designers and textile professionals curious about technical textiles, protective clothing and how extreme environments drive fabric innovation.

**You will learn to:**
- Explain why a spacesuit works as a wearable life-support system
- Name key spacesuit fibres and the property each one contributes
- Describe how suits manage outside heat and the astronaut's body heat
- Explain the trade-offs between protection, weight and mobility
- Identify features future space garments could offer on long missions

**Source:** this course is based on the Fibre2Fashion article [The Role of Textiles in Space Exploration](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/11885/the-role-of-textiles-in-space-exploration) by Diti Shah (Oct 2026).
