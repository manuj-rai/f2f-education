## Course page

**See why craft, detail and skilled making are becoming the quiet signals of luxury value.**

Luxury used to announce itself with a logo. Today, more of its value lies in details you have to look for. This free reading course is based on the Fibre2Fashion article by Kshipra Kulkarni, “The New Luxury Code: Craftsmanship, Detail and the Value of Making”. It explains why recognition is giving way to discernment and informed taste, and how time, individual character and rare skill create value in craft-led products. It ends with how designers can reinterpret heritage techniques, make artisans visible, use craft as a design language and combine it with technology.

**Who it is for:** Fashion and textile design students, designers, merchandisers and brand teams interested in craft, heritage textiles and how luxury value is changing.

**You will learn to:**
- Explain why luxury value is shifting from logos to craft and detail.
- Describe how textile knowledge and provenance shape a product’s perceived value.
- Analyse how time, individual character and rare skill create craft-led value.
- Reinterpret heritage techniques for contemporary products instead of reproducing them.
- Use craft as a design language, supported by technology and the maker’s story.

**Source:** this course is based on the Fibre2Fashion article [The New Luxury Code: Craftsmanship, Detail and the Value of Making](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/11903/the-new-luxury-code-craftsmanship-detail-and-the-value-of-making) by Kshipra Kulkarni (Nov 2026).
