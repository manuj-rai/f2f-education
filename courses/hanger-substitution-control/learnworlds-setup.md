## Course page

**A practical procedure to test, approve, trace and close a temporary hanger substitute.**

A supplier message arrives mid-season: the approved hanger clip is out of stock, but an alternative is available before the packing date. Should you approve it, and how? This free micro course, based on the Fibre2Fashion article by Frank Chan, gives apparel buyers a practical procedure. You will learn what to ask the supplier for, how to test a substitute on the garments most likely to expose a problem, how to approve a limited release, how to make the exception visible on cartons at receiving, and how to close the approval so the specification does not change silently.

**Who it is for:** Apparel buyers, sourcing and merchandising teams, quality controllers, and packing and warehouse staff who handle hanger changes during production.

**You will learn to:**
- Request the information and samples needed to judge a substitute hanger part.
- Set acceptance criteria and test substitutes on the garments most at risk.
- Approve a defined release limited by style, quantity, destination and shipment.
- Link the exception to cartons so receiving teams can check it.
- Close a temporary approval with an end condition and reconciliation.

**Source:** this course is based on the Fibre2Fashion article [How Apparel Buyers can Control Midseason Hanger Substitutions](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/11943/how-apparel-buyers-can-control-midseason-hanger-substitutions) by Frank Chan (Nov 2026).
