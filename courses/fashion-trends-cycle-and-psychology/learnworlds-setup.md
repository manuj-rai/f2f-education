## Course page

**Follow a trend from first idea to peak, decline and revival, and learn what drives it.**

Why does a look dominate one season and feel dated the next? This free reading course draws on two Fibre2Fashion articles by Kshipra Kulkarni: “The Fashion Cycle Explained: How Trends Rise, Peak and Decline” and “The Psychology and Business Behind Fashion Trends”. It follows a trend through the stages of the fashion cycle, explains the psychology of belonging and distinction that keeps trends moving, and shows how algorithms, creators, brands and retailers speed trends up and wear them out. It ends with what makes a trend last, why past styles return and how forecasters combine data with cultural insight.

**Who it is for:** Fashion design and merchandising students, designers, buyers, brand and retail teams, and anyone curious about why fashion trends come and go.

**You will learn to:**
- Describe the stages of the fashion cycle, from first idea to decline.
- Explain how culture and consumer psychology shape what people want to wear.
- Analyse how algorithms, creators and commercial decisions accelerate trends.
- Distinguish short-lived attention spikes from trends with lasting relevance.
- Explain why past styles return and how modern forecasting reads culture.

**Source:** this course is based on the Fibre2Fashion articles [The Fashion Cycle Explained: How Trends Rise, Peak and Decline](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/11893/the-fashion-cycle-explained-how-trends-rise-peak-and-decline) (Oct 2026) and [The Psychology and Business Behind Fashion Trends](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/11904/the-psychology-and-business-behind-fashion-trends) (Nov 2026) by Kshipra Kulkarni.
