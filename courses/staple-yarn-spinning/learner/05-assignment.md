# Live panel and applied assignment
This last section brings the week together. You will put everything from Units 1 to 4 to work in a one-page yarn recommendation for a real-world brief.

## The live panel
When this module runs with a cohort, the week closes with a 60-minute online panel and Q&A. The panel brings together a spinning-mill technical or commercial head, a sourcing or fabric lead from a brand or retailer, and an F2F Education host who chairs the session and adds market context. Cohort learners receive the date and joining link by email.

| Time | Segment | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 min | Welcome, recap of the week and a quick poll: which topic surprised you most? | Host |
| 5-20 min | The mill view: how a spinner decides what to make and how it prices yarn | Mill panellist |
| 20-30 min | The buyer view: how yarn choices are made, and mistakes to avoid | Brand panellist |
| 30-40 min | Market briefing: spinning capacity, fibre prices, and the shift towards compact and air-jet | Host |
| 40-55 min | Q&A, using questions from the Discuss tab and the live chat | All |
| 55-60 min | Assignment briefing and close | Host |

## Applied assignment: the yarn recommendation
**Task:** write a one-page yarn recommendation (about 500 words) for the brief below.

**Brief:** a mid-market menswear brand is launching a knitted polo shirt to sell at a mid-range retail price. It wants a soft handle, good shape retention after washing, low pilling and a credible sustainability story. The garment will be made in Bangladesh or India.

Your recommendation must cover:
1. Fibre or blend, with percentages
2. Yarn grade (carded, semi-combed or combed)
3. Count and ply, using a named count system
4. Spinning route
5. Expected performance against the brief: handle, shape retention and pilling
6. Cost position compared with the alternatives you rejected
7. Sustainability: which certification you would require, and one question you would ask the spinner

## How it is marked
The assignment is marked out of 100, and the pass mark is 60.

| Criterion | Marks | Top band | Pass band |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical accuracy | 30 | All terms used correctly; count, ply and route are consistent with each other | Mostly correct, with minor slips |
| Fit to brief | 25 | Every choice is clearly linked to a requirement in the brief | Choices are reasonable, but the links are implied, not stated |
| Trade-offs and cost | 20 | Names the rejected options and explains the cost and quality trade-off | Mentions cost without comparing alternatives |
| Sustainability | 15 | A specific certification and a sharp, relevant question for the supplier | A general sustainability statement |
| Clarity | 10 | Concise, structured and readable by a non-technical manager | Understandable but loose |

> **Tip:** Work through the seven points in order and give one reason for each choice. Then reread the brief and check that soft handle, shape retention, low pilling and the sustainability story are each answered.
