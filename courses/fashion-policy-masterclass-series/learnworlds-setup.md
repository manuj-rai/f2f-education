## Course page

**How EU green policy and producer responsibility laws are reshaping fashion, from design to sourcing.**

Fashion is moving from voluntary pledges to binding rules. This free masterclass series, based on the Fibre2Fashion articles by Arisekola Muritala and the Fibre2Fashion team, explains the policies now reshaping the industry. You will see how the EU Green Deal and Circular Economy Action Plan target textile waste, how Extended Producer Responsibility makes brands pay for the end of their products’ lives, and how EPR laws differ in France, the Netherlands, the EU and California. The final lessons turn policy into practice: designing for recycling, take-back programmes, the energy gap in producer countries, and the steps brands and suppliers can take now.

**Who it is for:** Brand, product and sourcing teams, suppliers exporting to Europe, and sustainability students who want a clear introduction to fashion and textile policy.

**You will learn to:**
- Explain why EU green policy targets textile waste and fast fashion.
- Describe how Extended Producer Responsibility shifts end-of-life costs to brands.
- Compare textile EPR schemes in France, the Netherlands, the EU and California.
- Apply eco-modulation thinking to material and product design choices.
- Identify practical steps brands and suppliers can take to prepare.

**Source:** this course is based on the Fibre2Fashion articles [EPR Laws: Fashion’s Next Challenge](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/10430/epr-laws-fashion-s-next-challenge) by Arisekola Muritala (May 2025) and [Green Policies in the EU: Advancements and Impacts](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/10162/green-policies-in-the-eu-advancements-and-impacts) by Fibre2Fashion (Oct 2024).
