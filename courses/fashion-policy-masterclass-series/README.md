# Fashion Policy Masterclass Series

A free **readable course** in LearnWorlds (`/course/fashion-policy-masterclass-series`), author **Sustainable Fashion School**.
Created on 25 Nov 2026 as a Coming-soon course; lessons added on 26 Nov 2026 at the user's request, built from two
Fibre2Fashion articles (see `content.json` → `source`, `source2`). Source of the lessons: `content.json` and `lessons/*.md`;
`python courses/_studio/reading.py fashion-policy-masterclass-series` rebuilds `upload/`, the quiz file and the card.
It was uploaded with `lw_publish_js.py fashion-policy-masterclass-series "Sustainable Fashion School" --existing`.
